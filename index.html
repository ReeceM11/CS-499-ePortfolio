Professional Self-Assessment

Throughout my time in the Computer Science program at Southern New Hampshire University, I have developed a stronger understanding of software development, databases, algorithms, and computer networks. Over the past three years, I have gained experience working with different programming languages and technologies while learning how these skills apply to real-world situations. My career interests have also become clearer, and I hope to pursue a career in cloud infrastructure, potentially working toward becoming a solutions architect.

One of the most important things I learned throughout the program was how different parts of a system work together. Projects such as my Travlr Getaway full-stack application helped me understand how front-end interfaces, back-end services, and databases communicate. I also gained experience with Android development, MongoDB, and networking. These projects strengthened my problem-solving skills and taught me the importance of writing organized, secure, and maintainable code. Working with algorithms and data structures also helped me understand how to process information more efficiently.

Although most of my coursework was completed individually, discussion posts provided opportunities to communicate with classmates, exchange ideas, and consider different approaches to solving problems. These discussions helped me improve how I explain technical concepts and respond to feedback. I also learned that security should be considered throughout the development process rather than added afterward, especially when working with authentication, user permissions, and databases.

For my capstone project, I enhanced my Travlr Getaway application in three areas: software design and engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases. These enhancements focused on improving application security, data processing, and database reliability. Together, they demonstrate my ability to review existing code, identify areas for improvement, and implement changes that make an application more dependable.

Looking back at where I started, I can see how much my confidence and problem-solving abilities have improved. Tasks that once seemed complicated have become easier to approach because I have learned how to break problems into smaller steps and work through them. I have also become more comfortable learning new technologies and troubleshooting issues on my own. I know there is still plenty for me to learn, especially in cloud infrastructure and networking, but I feel more confident in my ability to take on new challenges and continue growing professionally.
